End a one-task plan that has outgrown its task. Steps toward the brief's goal are yours: re-cut, give feedback, take a task over. Scope is not: when finishing needs a decision nobody made, a different problem than the brief describes, or work the brief does not cover, call `outgrown`, and name which in your report.

Review the open task first: accept what is done, or drop it. Then reply with your report: what is missing, what is done and committed, and what you propose. Whoever asked for the work decides what follows; the committed work is kept.

Only a one-task plan ends this way; a design's episode does not.
