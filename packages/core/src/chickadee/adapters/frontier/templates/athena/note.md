Keep a note for the rest of this design. `text` is required.

Appends one `note` line to the session's record, kept for as long as this design is being built.
Use it for what you worked out and will need later (why a task is cut where it is, a contract you
checked, a path that turned out not to matter), not for what the records already say. A note is
your claim, never a fact: the records win where they disagree.

A note over the size limit is refused with `INVALID_ARGUMENTS`, and nothing is written.
